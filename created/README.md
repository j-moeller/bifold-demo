# Created characters

The character creator saves the characters you make here, a folder per
character (`character.json` and the textures painted for it). The demo puts
them into the fair, each in the place of one of the fair's own people who
walk about (the visitors who walk up to the camera first), and notices new,
changed or deleted ones within a second (`--created DIR` for another folder).
`C` in the demo opens the creator. See "Character creator" and "Created
characters" in the main README.

They can hold a person's face (from photos), which is personal data: this
folder stays on this computer and is ignored by git. Deleting a character's
folder removes everything of it at once; its 3D person leaves the fair at the
next moment nobody sees it go (press `R` in the demo to make that now).
